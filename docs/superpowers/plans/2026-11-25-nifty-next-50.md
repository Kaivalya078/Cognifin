# Phase 5.5 NIFTY Next 50 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Grow the corpus from the NIFTY 50 (47 companies today) to the NIFTY 100. Each new company gets its FY2025 and FY2026 annual reports plus 7 quarters in the financials table. Then re-run the evals and update the copy.

**Architecture:** This is mostly data work through the existing Phase 3 pipeline:
- discovery
- `financials.sources` and `financials.build`
- `ingestion.batch_ingest_annual_reports`, which appends to the registry
- the evals

Code changes are small:
- a membership snapshot plus a check
- aliases
- the landing check and the copy
- golden and key additions

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.13 (`backend/venv/Scripts/python`, CUDA torch for indexing), LanceDB, DuckDB, React frontend. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-nifty-next-50-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- **Sources:**
  - Public only: `nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ixbrl/…_iXBRL_WEB.html`, company investor-relations PDFs, and BSE `AttachHis` PDFs whose links are resolved through the user's logged-in Screener session.
  - **No NSE or BSE JSON list endpoints, and no paid feeds.**
  - Fetch at 1 request per second with backoff, and resumable.
- **Depth for new companies:** `backend/data/<SYM>/2025.pdf` and `2026.pdf`, plus the quarters ending 2025-03-31 through 2026-09-30 (7 quarters).
- **R2 budget (hard check):** the sum of annual-report PDF sizes plus the size of the `index_cache` tarball must be under 10 GB. If it isn't, stop and ask.
- **OpenAI spend cap: about $0.60 for the phase.**
  - Log every paid step's cost in the ledger.
  - Stop and ask if the next step would cross the cap.
- **Regression gate:** on the 100 v2 golden cases, NIFTY 50 retrieval hit@3 must stay at or above 0.773 and answer correctness at or above 0.850 (the v3 values 0.793 and 0.870, minus 2 points). If either fails, stop and ask.
- **Paths and commands:**
  - Use `backend/venv/Scripts/python` (bare `python` is miniconda, which has no ruff).
  - Prefix Python runs with `PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8`.
  - Backend suite: `python -m pytest tests/test_*.py -q`.
  - Lint: `ruff check .`
- **Frontend checks:** `npm run lint`, every `src/utils/*.check.mjs`, and `VITE_API_BASE=https://api.example.invalid npm run build`.
- **Git:** PDFs, `index_cache/`, `.superpowers/` and the quarterly folders stay out of git.

## Review Focus

1. **A near-duplicate company name** ("Bajaj Housing Finance" vs "Bajaj Finance", "Tata Power" vs "Tata Steel", "Adani Green" vs "Adani Enterprises") must resolve to the right symbol, never the shorter match (Task 1 test).
2. **A question about a pre-FY2025 year for a Next 50 company** must be answered from the FY2025 report's prior-year column with a citation, not refused, and not answered from another company (Task 6, golden case kind `table` with `year` 2024).
3. **A company that has published only one annual report** (newly listed) is indexed with what exists, without a crash or a phantom year, and appears in the count (Task 2 check script).
4. **A results PDF that is image-only, or whose figures are in Lakhs.** Its values must be scaled correctly or dropped, never stored ×100 wrong. The new key cases include at least 1 Lakhs filing and 1 PDF-only filing (Task 5).
5. **The NIFTY 50 answers after the corpus doubles.** A NIFTY 50 question must not cite a Next 50 document (Task 6 regression gate, plus a scripted check that every cited `chunk_id` in the v2 run belongs to the asked company).
